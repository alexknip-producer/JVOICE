What's inside

One organism, a species per engine

Each engine is a species, drawn by the firmware itself. On HOME every track is a creature of its engine's species: its shape comes from its knobs, and it swells and heats up with its own audio.

Eleven of the twelve engines (EXT plays your audio), as the FM-1 draws them

Aengines

12 engines

ANALOG, FM6 (six operators, 32 algorithms, DX7 banks), PHASE, MATH (bytebeat and floatbeat), VOICE (it sings words in Spanish, English and Japanese), WHEEL, NOISE, ANIMAL (birds, insects and frogs from physical models), STRING (a plucked, struck or bowed string, and guitar feedback), ADD (additive) and DRUM-X: no samples, everything is synthesized. And EXT, which plays a USB audio input on a track of its own (or SWARM, a melody out of the Bluetooth around): gated by the steps, ring-modulated, crushed, and followed as a modulation source.

Bheart

DRUM-X

A drum kit of eight synthesized sounds, each with two sides: MORPH moves the whole kit from one to the other, live. The EXTENDED key map gives every octave a job: real fills above, the kick, the tom or any sound played chromatically below, ratchets and flams. A classic CMOS cowbell, kicks that punch.

Cnervous system

64-step sequencer

A piano roll per track, chords, probability, ratchets, extra playheads, recording off the grid to a 64th of a step, and EUCLID, which writes euclidean patterns live: a whole drum kit from one DENSITY knob or lane by lane, melodies in key, VARY turns that answer each other, and MUTATE, which evolves the pattern on the bar.

Dmembrane

Effects and master

Delay (its right echo can take a time of its own), five reverbs (SHIMMER, RESO, CLOUD…), chorus, slicer, live punch-in effects, a DJ filter on the whole mix and a master with limiter, clip, punch, tape and a 4-band EQ.

Ememory

Songs and the generator

Six songs of four parts each: TIMELINE draws the song and opens each row track by track (another part, a variation, a mute), PARTS loops one part, CLIPS launches parts track by track on the next bar. And GENE, a generator that writes a whole song idea: from a genre (cumbia, phonk, baile funk, hip hop, jazz…), or in EXPLORE, breaking every rule.

Froots

USB audio and MIDI

The FM-1 becomes an audio interface that keeps its time: every track on its own channel into your DAW (STEMS), ready to record each part on its own, and the computer's audio back in (IN+OUT). MIDI over USB, TRS and Bluetooth: play it from a phone or tablet app with no cable, and PROX turns how close the phone is into a modulation source. USB HOST (experimental): a USB-MIDI keyboard or a USB audio interface plugged straight in.

Find your way

Six rules, one map

Since 0.9.2 every section works the same way: learn six rules once and the whole synth is known, in any of the four skins (AIR, COLORS, BOTTOM, HUD). The map shows each button's groups and pages in the order SELECT walks them. It was drawn at 0.9.18 and V10 has moved some pages since (SEQ opens on STEP and has EUCLID, SAVE opens on TIMELINE, GLO has COMPS, EDIT ends with VOICE): the interactive manual's map follows V10.

1SELECT

SELECT walks the pages

One chain per section, a page a detent; it stops at the first and the last page.

2button

The button: in, or on

From another section, the page you left there. Inside, the first page of the next group, round.

3PRESETS

PRESETS loads presets

The track's sounds everywhere, the patterns in SEQ, the genre in GEN. Never a page or a track.

4KNOB 1

KNOB 1 picks

The step, the bar, the slot, the stage, the kit's sound. The track is always ALGORITHM's.

5hold

Hold = the layer

A button held puts its shortcuts on the keys: SEQ + G5 clears a step, SAVE + C5 names, REC held alone undoes (again: redo). EDIT + PRESETS jumps between engines; held + SELECT: GEN, the dice, the tempo.

6screen

The screen says where

Tabs on top are the section's groups, a dot a page; the footer says SECTION · GROUP · page and n/N.

FAQ

Before you install

Can I go back to the official firmware?

Yes. The installer has a button to return to the official firmware, V16 or V15 (you choose M-VAVE's official FM-1.fwsc). It saves a complete backup first.

Which browser do I need?

Chrome or Edge on a computer, for the installer and the editor: they talk to the FM-1 over USB. The emulator also works in other browsers and on phones.

Will I lose my sounds or projects?

Back them up with the editor before installing or changing version. The installer can also install earlier versions, but a project saved by a newer version may not open right in an older one. This version keeps 6 songs (8 before): songs 7 and 8 stay only in a backup made before updating.

The installer doesn't find my FM-1.

Use a USB cable that carries data (many cables only charge), connect it straight to the computer without a hub, and close other programs that use the FM-1 over MIDI.
